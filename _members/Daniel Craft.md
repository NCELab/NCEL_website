Daniel is a Clinical Psychology PhD student at Fairleigh Dickinson University whose work focuses on trauma, psychopathology, affective processes, and cognitive-emotional regulation. For the past six years, he has been a member of the NeuroCognition and Emotion Lab, where he currently serves as the Research Coordinator. In this role, he has managed large-scale research projects examining PTSD, intergenerational trauma, cognition, and emotional functioning, including studies involving World Trade Center responder families. He has contributed to multiple peer-reviewed publications and presentations at national and international conferences, including the Association for Psychological Science and the International Society for Traumatic Stress Studies. His broader focus is on the goal of integrating rigorous empirical research with evidence-based clinical practice.
