Dr. Rodriguez-Moreno obtained her degree in Biology from the University of Buenos Aires, Argentina, and later her Ph.D. in Neurosciences at Weill Cornell Graduate School of Medical Sciences. 

Her research uses neuroimaging approaches, including task-based and resting state fMRI, and behavioral and neuroeconomic methods, to investigate the neural mechanisms underlying cognition and emotion across healthy and clinical populations. Her early work examined cognitive function and residual brain function following severe brain injury, including in patients with disorders of consciousness. Her current research focuses on how trauma, stress, and emotional experiences affect brain function and behavior across the lifespan, with particular emphasis on emotion regulation, decision-making, and risk-taking in adolescents and trauma-exposed populations. Her goal is to identify brain patterns associated with resilience and vulnerability to trauma to inform early interventions and promote resilience among individuals facing significant life adversity.
