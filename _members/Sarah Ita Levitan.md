Dr. Sarah Ita Levitan's research focuses on speech and natural language processing, with an emphasis on understanding how people communicate information beyond the words they use. She develops computational methods to analyze acoustic-prosodic and linguistic cues in spoken interactions, exploring how they convey social and psychological information such as trust and deception. Her work combines machine learning, speech processing, and natural language processing to better understand human communication and develop socially aware AI systems.
