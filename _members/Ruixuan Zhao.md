Ruixuan received her M.A. in Quantitative Methods in the Social Sciences from Columbia University, with a concentration in Data Science, and her B.A. in Psychology from Barnard College. As a research assistant in the NCEL, she contributes to multiple research projects, overseeing data collection, leading data analyses, and developing computational approaches to investigate cognition and mental health. Neuroimaging is central to her research interest, which focuses on the neural mechanisms underlying memory, emotion, and cognitive control, with a particular emphasis on quantitative approaches to understanding how these processes contribute to adaptive and maladaptive behavior.
