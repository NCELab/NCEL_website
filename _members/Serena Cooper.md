Serena Cooper is a senior at Columbia University studying Cognitive Science. She is interested in clinical neuropsychology, particularly the relationship between trauma, memory, and cognitive functioning. Her research interests focus on understanding how traumatic experiences shape brain-behavior processes across development and influence long-term mental health outcomes. Serena plans to pursue a Ph.D. in clinical neuropsychology and ultimately become a practicing clinical neuropsychologist combining research and clinical care. Outside of the lab, Serena loves hiking, spending time outdoors, and exploring new places in New York City.
