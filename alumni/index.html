Sydney Lee Alumni University of Nevada, Las Vegas

Sydney Lee received her M.A. in Clinical Psychology from Teachers College, Columbia University. A former professional ballet dancer and actor, she focuses on performing arts psychology, with particular interest in supporting artists with sensory sensitivities, including ADHD, in high-performance settings. As part of NCEL, she helps manage participant relations and assists with session data collection for the EEG and ADHD study. Sydney was recently admitted to her first-choice PhD program in clinical psychology at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, where she will conduct research under the mentorship of Dr. Bradley Donohue in the Optimal Performance Program in Sports Lab (TOPPS).
