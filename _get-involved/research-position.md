# Research Position
 
## Join the the lab
{: #Join the the lab }

We welcome students, postdoctoral fellows, and researchers at different stages of their careers who are interested in cognitive neuroscience, emotion, development, trauma, and mental health and whose research interests align with the work of the NCEL.
We value curiosity, collaboration, and interdisciplinary approaches to research. Trainees in the lab have opportunities to contribute to ongoing studies, develop their own research questions, and gain experience with behavioral, neurophysiological, neuroimaging, and computational methods.
If you are interested in joining the lab, please send a brief description of your research interests and how they align with our work, along with your CV or résumé.

## Collaborations
{: #Collaborations }

We welcome collaborations with researchers whose expertise and interests intersect with our research on cognition and emotion, trauma and resilience, neurodevelopment, psychiatric disorders, and mental health.
Our collaborative work brings together behavioral and clinical research with EEG, MEG, fMRI, computational approaches, and innovative methods for studying cognition, emotion, and real-world experiences. We are particularly interested in interdisciplinary collaborations that can contribute new perspectives, methods, or populations to our ongoing research programs.
If you are interested in collaborating with the NCEL, please contact us by email to discuss potential research projects.
