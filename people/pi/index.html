## Yael Cycowicz (PI)
Yael M. Cycowicz principal-investigator Columbia University / New York State Psychiatric Institute

Dr. Cycowicz is a cognitive neuroscientist with a strong foundation in neurophysiological research, employing methodologies such as MEG, EEG, and fMRI in combination with diverse experimental designs and advanced analytic approaches. Her work examines the dynamic relationships between brain activity and behavior, with a particular interest in neuroplasticity and how cognitive functions develop and change across the lifespan, from childhood through older adulthood. More recently, her research has expanded to investigate the interactions between cognition and emotion and how these processes are reshaped by exposure to trauma and by psychiatric conditions, including attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), substance use disorders (SUD), anxiety, and depression.
