Daniel Craft

Fairleigh Dickinson University/ Columbia University / New York State Psychiatric Institute

About

Daniel is a Clinical Psychology PhD student at Fairleigh Dickinson University. He has been a member of the NCEL for several years and currently serves as Research Coordinator. In this role, he manages large-scale research projects involving World Trade Center responder and their families, with a focus on the intergenerational transmission of trauma. His scientific interests center on the impact of psychological trauma on the development of psychiatric disorders, particularly PTSD, as well as its comorbidity with mood and anxiety disorders, including depression. His broader goal is to integrate rigorous empirical research on trauma-related psychopathology with evidence-based clinical practice.
