About

Max is a second -year master’s student in the Clinical Psychology program at Teachers College with a concentration in Forensic Psychology. Their research interests include wartime sexual violence through an intersectional lens, conflict resolution in Israel/Palestine and other conflict-affected regions, and reducing sexual violence within carceral systems, with a particular focus on its disproportionate impact on queer incarcerated individuals.
