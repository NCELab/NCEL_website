Kelly Wang

intern

Columbia University / New York State Psychiatric Institute

Kelly is a second -year M.A. student in the Clinical Psychology program at Teachers College, Columbia University. Her work in the NCEL lab focuses on data management and EEG preprocessing. She is deeply passionate about leveraging neuroimaging techniques (EEG, fMRI) within experimental paradigms to investigate how adverse or traumatic experiences shape cognitive functioning and emotional processing. Grounded in this perspective, she aims to advance research that integrates biological, psychological, and experiential levels of analysis to inform more culturally responsive, accessible, and neuroscience-informed mental health interventions, with the long-term goal of pursuing a PhD in Clinical Psychology.
