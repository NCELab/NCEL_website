ENM

Emotion & Memory Study

Trauma Emotion Memory EEG MRI Neuroimaging Anxiety

The Emotion and Memory (E&M) Study investigates how childhood exposure to trauma influences the neural systems involved in emotion and memory processing later in life. The study focuses on individuals exposed to the events of September 11 during childhood or adolescence and examines how early trauma may alter cognitive and emotional functioning in adulthood. Using EEG, MRI, and behavioral tasks, E&M explores brain activity and connectivity related to emotional regulation, memory, and anxiety. The project aims to identify neurobiological pathways associated with long-term trauma exposure and improve understanding of mental health trajectories across development.
