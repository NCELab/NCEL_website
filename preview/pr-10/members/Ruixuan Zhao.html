About

Ruixuan Zhao received her M.A. in Quantitative Methods in the Social Sciences from Columbia University with a concentration in Data Science and her B.A. in Psychology from Barnard College. As a research assistant at the NeuroCognition and Emotion Lab, she has contributed to projects examining trauma, cognition, and mental health outcomes. Her research interests focus on trauma and human cognition, particularly the neural mechanisms underlying memory, emotion, and cognitive control, with an interest in applying neuroimaging and computational approaches to better understand mental health and behavior.
