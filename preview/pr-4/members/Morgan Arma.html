About

Morgan is a graduate student at Teachers College, Columbia University, where she is pursuing a master’s degree in psychology. Her academic and research interests include clinical psychology, trauma and resilience, suicide prevention, substance use treatment, and the ways social and interpersonal factors shape mental health. She is especially interested in working with individuals and communities affected by adversity, including veterans, adolescents, and people navigating anxiety, depression, or substance use concerns. In the future, Morgan hopes to pursue a doctoral-level degree in psychology and continue building her clinical and research skills as she works toward becoming a clinical psychologist
