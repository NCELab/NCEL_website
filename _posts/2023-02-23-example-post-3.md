9/11 Responders’ Trauma May Have Affected Their Children, Study Finds
date: 2026-09-02
author: Jasmine Laws
external_url: https://www.newsweek.com/9-11-responders-trauma-may-have-affected-their-children-study-12393826
---
Twenty-five years after the September 11 attacks, a study has found the children of World Trade Center responders may have been impacted by their parents' trauma, even though they did not see the destruction themselves.

The study, conducted by researchers at Columbia University, found the children of 9/11 responders who developed post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) after the terrorist attacks were more likely to have psychological conditions, such as anxiety and depression.
